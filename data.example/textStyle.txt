I build automation systems for small businesses. My work is identifying where manual processes are bleeding time or money and replacing them with systems that just run.

Writing style: direct, specific, no fluff. I don't write essays. I write thoughts. Short sentences. Sometimes just one line. I don't explain the obvious.

My audience: business owners tired of operational chaos, burned by consultants who "digitized" nothing, who've hired people when a system would have cost less.

I'm not a motivational speaker. I'm a builder. Posts should feel like something I'd say to a client across a table — not something from a marketing deck.

Things I care about: operational clarity, systems that don't require babysitting, fixing the actual root cause instead of hiring someone to manage the symptom.

Things that annoy me: fake AI hype, "productivity tips" that solve nothing, businesses running on one person's memory.
